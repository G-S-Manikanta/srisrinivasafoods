Meet Our Chef

With a passion towards cooking, I Srinivas G R started my journey as a chef. Cooking is something that makes me happy and I love cooking for others. I always wanted to be a chef and I really worked hard. It is a very nice profession where you can learn new things daily. When I serve a bowl of a recipe, it contains my love, hard work, and passion for my profession.

"Food is something that gives us nutrition, and all of us know how nutritious it is to have bitter gourd. But there are very few of us who like it and most children run away when their mother cooks bitter gourd. So, how can a mother convince them to eat? All she can do is cook it in such a way that their child would love to eat it. It is not less than magic to turn bitter gourd into a delicious recipe. And this chef is a person who has a perfect solution for this."

At Sri Srinivasa Foods, we bring that same culinary magic and deep reverence into every batch of pickles, freshly ground spice powders, crispy sun-dried sandiges, and authentic tokku. Rooted in traditional Karnataka Brahmin vegetarian culinary heritage, every recipe is crafted purely with natural ingredients, cold-pressed oils, and age-old sun-drying techniques.
